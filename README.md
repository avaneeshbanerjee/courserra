# courserra

simple intrest calculator

Core Variables Required
Principal (\(P\)): The initial amount borrowed or invested.
Interest Rate (\(r\)): The annual percentage charged or earned.
Time (\(t\)): The loan or investment duration in years.
The Mathematical Formulas
Interest Amount (\(I\)): Calculated as \(I = P \times r \times t\).
Total Balance (\(A\)): Calculated as \(A = P + I\).
Simple vs. Compound Interest
Feature	Simple Interest	Compound Interest
Calculation Base	Original principal amount only.	Principal plus accumulated interest.
Growth Pattern	Linear and constant growth.	Exponential and accelerating growth.
